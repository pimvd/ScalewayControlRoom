Scaleway ControlRoom — Privacy policy

The developer collects no data. There is no ControlRoom server, account, analytics or tracking.

Your API keys

The API keys you enter are stored in your iPhone's keychain. They are sent only to Scaleway's API (api.scaleway.com), to list and control your instances and to read your costs. Removing a key in the app deletes it from your iPhone.

Your Scaleway data

Instance details and costs are fetched straight from Scaleway to your iPhone and shown there. They are not sent anywhere else. Scaleway's own privacy policy covers what Scaleway does with your requests.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; the developer receives no personal or payment details.
