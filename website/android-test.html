Test ControlRoom for Android

ControlRoom starts and stops Scaleway servers and shows what they cost, from your phone. Before Google lets a new app into the Play Store, at least 12 people have to test it for 14 days. Could you be one of them? It takes two minutes to join; after that, all you do is keep the app installed.

What you need

  • An Android phone (Android 9 or later) with the Play Store.
  • A Google account, the one you use on that phone.
  • No Scaleway account: the app has a demo with made-up servers and costs, and every feature is open in it.

How to join

  1. Join the tester group with the Google account on your phone:
    Join the tester group
  2. Then accept the invitation to the test, with the same account:
    Become a tester
  3. On that page, follow the link to Google Play and install ControlRoom. It is free.
  4. Open it and tap Try the demo.
  5. Keep the app installed for 14 days, and open it now and then. That is what Google counts.

Things to try

  • Stop a server and start it again, and watch its state change.
  • Open a serverless app and look at its logs and its metrics.
  • Look up last month's costs, by category and by project.
  • In Other, search for a database and open it.
  • Have a Scaleway account? Add an API key (Settings → Add API key) and try it on your own servers.

Feedback

Anything odd, unclear or broken: email pim@vandijkcloud.nl, or leave private feedback on the app's Play Store page (only the developer sees it). A screenshot helps. "It all works" is useful to hear too.

Good to know

  • The app collects nothing about you. Its only connection is to Scaleway's API, and only with a key you add. See the privacy policy.
  • After the 14 days you can keep the app, or uninstall it and leave the group.
  • ControlRoom is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Scaleway.
